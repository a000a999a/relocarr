Finding your X-Plex-Token

  1. Sign in to your Plex account in the Plex web app.
  2. Browse to any library item and choose "View XML" for it.
  3. Look at the resulting URL's address bar: it ends in ...&X-Plex-Token=<value>. Copy only the <value> part after the = into the field above -- not the X-Plex-Token= part itself (a common copy-paste slip; relocarr now strips it automatically if pasted that way, but the token itself still needs to be the real one).

Full walkthrough: Plex Support -- Finding an authentication token / X-Plex-Token.
