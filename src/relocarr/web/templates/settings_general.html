General

Caps how fast each file copy writes to the destination. 10 is conservative -- raised to protect a RAM-constrained destination from being overwhelmed by a sustained large write (confirmed live: can OOM-kill the destination's own SMB server). Raise it if your destination can take it; lower it if it can't. Applies to every app's transfers, since it protects whatever's actually receiving the writes.
